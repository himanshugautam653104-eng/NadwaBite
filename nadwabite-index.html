Our story

A local delivery startup, built from Nadwasarai

NadwaBite is a local food-ordering and home-delivery startup started by Himanshu Gautam. We don't run a restaurant or kitchen — instead, we take your order, arrange the food fresh from trusted local sellers and the market nearby, and deliver it straight to your home by cycle.

It's a simple idea: give people in Nadwasarai and around Mau an easy way to order the fast food they love, without needing to step out.
